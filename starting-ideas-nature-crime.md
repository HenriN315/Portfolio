# Starting Ideas 1/20/2026
## What is this Project?
This project, whatever it is, stems from at least three places:
1. A desire to professionally break into the conservation space
2. A desire to contribute to something that advances sustainability, equity, and justice
3. A desire to familiarize myself with the cultural and political landscape of Latin America, specifically Mexico

To me, a project on nature crime seems like an area of intersection between these three things. What I mean when I say nature crime, at least as of right now, I use the definition given in [this report](https://www.climateandforests2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/CRIME_Understanding-and-Tackling-a-Key-Threat_BarberWinfieldPetersen.pdf) by Charles Victor Barber, Karen Winfield, and Rachael Peterson of _Climate and Forests 2030_.
